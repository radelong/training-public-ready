# Active Directory training courses

The Training Academy catalog now includes two structured courses in on-premises Active Directory Domain Services:

- **Active Directory: Foundations and Operations** — seven modules, 70 instructional slides, 35 module-quiz questions, and a 34-question final exam.
- **Active Directory: Advanced Administration and Troubleshooting** — six modules, 60 instructional slides, 30 module-quiz questions, and a 30-question final exam.

Both courses use an 80% passing score, up to three attempts, randomized question order on retakes, and answer explanations. The Foundation and Operations course is estimated at 475 minutes; the Advanced course is estimated at 470 minutes.

The public source repository contains no course slides, exams, answer keys, or learner records. Protected course content is delivered through restricted instructor packages and stored in the authenticated Academy.

HOW TRAINING WORKS

One place for instruction and practice

Training Academy supports technical learning through class modules, practical lab sessions, and recorded progress. Access to the actual classes, lab machines, and results requires an Academy account.

Classes build the foundation

Class modules present concepts in a planned order. Learners review training material, mark completed modules, and take a final exam. Their account keeps class progress so they can return to the material.

Labs turn knowledge into action

When a learner starts an available lab, the Academy assigns a private virtual machine and opens its terminal in the browser. Guided exercises ask the learner to perform work in that environment and check the outcome. Lab sessions can be reset for another attempt.

Practice stays separate from production

Training work belongs in a controlled environment. Learners can explore, troubleshoot, and repeat procedures without relying on a production system for practice. Administrators manage access and review training results.

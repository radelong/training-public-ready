# Reviewed public source scope

This is a separate source copy. Original repositories, histories, deployments, identities, user records, uploaded content and recovery keys are not included. There are no inherited Git commits. Setup uses demonstration identities and configuration; supply your own local settings.

Actual course PDFs, exam banks, instructor answer keys, operational notes, screenshots, uploaded documents/photos, private hosting identities and deployment binaries are deliberately excluded. Training includes only platform/tooling source and generic authored practice exercises, not the private course-content bundle. Third-party dependencies are fetched separately under their own licenses; required bundled-source notices are preserved. First-party source has no newly assigned open-source license.

Do not point tests or installers at existing live services. Backups of the original content remain private. Review results and validation evidence are recorded separately.
